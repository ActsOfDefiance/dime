# Dime Product Discovery Questions

*Saved from initial product management conversation*

## Vision & Goals
- What's the actual vision for Dime? Is this for Acts of Defiance specifically or a broader audience?
- What problem are you trying to solve that existing content creation tools don't address?
- Who is your target user - activists, researchers, general public, or something else?

## Current Context
- Why did you import the podcast documentation - are you pivoting from an existing project?
- What's driving the timeline? Do you have a specific launch target or funding deadline?
- Who else is involved in this project besides you?

## User Needs
- Have you talked to anyone who would actually use this tool?
- What's their current workflow for creating content about liberation movements?
- What are their biggest pain points with existing tools?

## Technical Constraints
- What's your technical background/preference? Are you comfortable with the full FastAPI stack?
- Do you have hosting budget/preferences (cloud vs. self-hosted)?
- Are there any compliance or security requirements given the political nature?

## Scope & Priorities
- Is the political/liberation movement focus firm, or are you open to broader content creation?
- What does "success" look like for v1 - usage metrics, user feedback, revenue?
- What features are you most excited about vs. what you think users need?
